# AI Pipeline & Speech Processing

Bu modul sun'iy intellekt xizmatlari va modellarini o'z ichiga oladi:
- **Speech-to-Text (STT)**: O'zbek tili va aralash nutq (Uzbek + Russian + English) uchun moslashtirilgan Whisper / NeMo modellari.
- **LLM Reasoning & Explanation**: Ma'ruzalar va uchrashuvlarni tushuntirish, umumlashtirish (summarization).
- **Diarization & Audio Preprocessing**: So'zlovchilarni ajratish, shovqinni kamaytirish va audio formatlash.
